PYQ COLLECTION STARTER KIT (2000-2026)

This ZIP contains a curated list of official source websites, a CSV template for direct PDF URLs, and a downloader script. It DOES NOT contain the question paper PDFs themselves.

1. Open sources.csv to visit exam authorities' official archives.
2. Copy public direct PDF links into pdf_urls.csv, adding category, exam, year, and filename for each row.
3. Run: python download_pdfs.py
4. Successfully downloaded PDFs will be organised under papers/category/exam/year.
5. To ZIP the downloaded PDFs later, use your operating system's compression tool.

Important: Some exams did not exist in their current form in 2000. Availability depends on each authority's archive. This kit does not bypass restricted downloads or claim to include a complete archive. CBSE is the included board starting point; UP Board and CISCE source websites are also listed.
